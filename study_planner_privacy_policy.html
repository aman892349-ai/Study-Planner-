Privacy Policy

Study Planner – Focus on Learning

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Study Planner – Focus on Learning (“the app”) handles information when you use it.

At a glance: The app is designed to work offline. Study tasks, schedules, notes, subjects, preferences, and focus-session records are intended to be stored locally on your device. No account or OTP login is required.

1. Information You Enter

You may enter study tasks, subjects, schedules, notes, focus-session records, and app preferences. This information supports the app’s planning and tracking features. These records are designed to remain on your device and are not sent by us to a server. Avoid entering sensitive personal information into notes or other fields.

2. Accounts and Authentication

The app does not require an account, phone number, or OTP authentication to use its core features.

3. Internet, Analytics, and Advertising

The current version is intended to operate offline and does not intentionally send study records to a remote server. No advertising SDK, including Google AdMob, is intended to be included in this version. We do not intentionally use third-party analytics or tracking services in this version.

If a future version introduces online services, analytics, advertising, or other third-party tools, this policy will be updated to describe the relevant data practices.

4. Notifications and Reminders

If you enable study reminders, the app may schedule local notifications on your device. Availability depends on your Android version and permissions. You can disable notifications in Android settings or within the app, where available.

5. Storage, Retention, and Deletion

App information is intended to be stored locally on your device. You can edit or delete information using available in-app controls. Uninstalling the app may delete locally stored data. Device backup settings and Android behavior may affect whether app data is backed up.

We cannot recover locally stored information if it is deleted or lost. Keep your own copy of any information that is important to you.

6. Children’s Privacy

Study Planner is a general study and productivity tool. It does not intentionally collect personal information through an account system. If you believe information has been transmitted to us unintentionally, contact us using the details below.

7. Security

We aim to minimize data collection by keeping core study information on the device. However, no electronic storage method is guaranteed to be completely secure. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep your operating system updated.

8. Your Choices

  • Choose what information you enter.
  • Manage or delete information using available in-app controls.
  • Control notification permissions through Android settings.
  • Stop using the app and uninstall it at any time.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if app features or data practices change. The updated version will be published at the same URL with a revised “Last updated” date.
